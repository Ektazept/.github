## About Ektazept

Ektazept is a software and SaaS startup focused on building useful digital products and services. We are an early-stage team of four Co-Founders, and our product portfolio is still taking shape.

We care about doing the fundamentals well: understanding real problems, building thoughtfully, and improving steadily over time.

> [!NOTE]
> *Ekta* is how we sometimes refer to ourselves casually. **Ektazept** is our official name.

<br />
